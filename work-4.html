Bigelow Tea · Client capstone project, MK689 Digital Marketing CapstoneStrategy

Digital marketing plan for Bigelow Botanicals Cold Water Infusion

The project
Bigelow Tea was the client for my graduate digital marketing capstone. The brief: create a social and digital marketing plan to build awareness and e-commerce sales for its Botanicals cold water infusion on Amazon and bigelowtea.com, with a $50K budget. I built the plan and pitched it as a deck.
What I did
  • Researched the market: compared Botanicals with 6 competitors (Celestial, Twinings, Cirkul, Hint, Liquid I.V. and MiO) on social presence and Amazon ratings.
  • Ran keyword research and found that shoppers searching for "flavored water" and "water enhancer" were finding competitors, not Bigelow.
  • Recommended the shift: stop selling it as iced tea and position it as better flavored water, to win new water drinkers.
  • Created the "6 Flavors, 6 Days" creator campaign for TikTok and Instagram, with Facebook offers and Amazon Subscribe & Save to turn trial into repeat buying.
  • Planned the year-round calendar, the $50K budget split and the KPIs to track reach, repeat purchase and rating.
